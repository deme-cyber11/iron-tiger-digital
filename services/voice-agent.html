What It Does

Everything a Receptionist Does. Nothing They Don't.

Trained on hundreds of real home service calls, our AI handles every inbound conversation the way a trained employee would — consistently, professionally, around the clock.

Answers Every Call Instantly

No hold time, no voicemail, no "press 1 for this." The agent picks up on the first ring with a friendly, professional greeting — just like you would. Whether it's 2 PM or 2 AM, the call is answered.

Qualifies the Job

The agent asks the right questions — what service is needed, where the job is, what the timeline looks like, and any specifics relevant to your trade. You get a pre-qualified lead, not just a name and number.

Delivers the Lead by Text

Within 30 seconds of the call ending, you get a text with everything: the caller's name, phone number, job description, address, and any urgency notes. You're always first to call back — before they try a competitor.

Handles Unlimited Calls at Once

Busy season? Your agent doesn't get overwhelmed. While a human receptionist handles one call, our AI answers all of them simultaneously — every caller gets immediate, personal attention no matter how many calls come in.

Common Questions

AI Voice Agent FAQs

Does the AI voice agent sound robotic?+

No. Our agents use natural conversational AI with realistic voices, trained specifically on real home service calls. Most callers don't realize they're speaking with an AI until the agent tells them — and we're transparent about it when asked.

What happens after the agent captures a lead?+

Within 30 seconds of the call ending, you receive a text message with the caller's name, phone number, job description, and any other details the agent captured. You call them back — you're always first because you were never "missed."

Can the AI handle multiple calls at once?+

Yes. Unlike a human receptionist, our AI handles unlimited simultaneous inbound calls. Whether one person calls or fifty, every call is answered immediately with no hold time and no call waiting.

What if a caller asks something the AI can't answer?+

The agent is trained to capture the caller's information and let them know you'll be in touch shortly. For anything outside its scope, it gracefully hands off rather than guessing — and you're notified immediately with everything that was captured.

How long does setup take?+

Setup typically takes 3–5 business days. We configure the agent with your business name, services, pricing ranges, and service area — then run internal test calls before going live. Most contractors are up and running within a week.
