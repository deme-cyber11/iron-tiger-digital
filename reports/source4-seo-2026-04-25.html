Finding 4: Internal Search Rate is a Red Flag

/search is the #2 page on the site by Clarity sessions (11,277). 4,401 sessions trigger an on-site search event. Average pages per session: 1.68. The information architecture is failing to route buyers by intent — users land, can't find what they need, and search.

Action: Pull Shopify top-20 internal search queries (TG provides). Build "Shop by Application" mega-menu: Warehouse Safety | Lubrication Systems | Material Handling | Loading Dock | Lifting Equipment. Add cross-sell blocks on top 10 product pages. Target: /search session rate drops 20–30% in 30 days with zero new traffic.

Finding 5: Bing Buyers Convert at 2.2× Google Buyers

Bing organic has 7.8% of Google's session volume but generates 21.4% of Google's organic revenue (1.25% vs 0.56% CVR). Bing CPC, conversely, is the worst-performing paid channel at 0.44% — half of Google CPC on $368K spend-equivalent.

Actions (combined <4 hours): (1) Submit updated sitemap to Bing Webmaster Tools. (2) Install Shopify IndexNow plugin — pings Bing on every publish. (3) Audit Bing CPC keyword list and match types — 0.44% CVR suggests a mismatch costing real money.
