Common Questions

How quickly will I see results?
Your dedicated website is already live and indexed in Google — you're not waiting for us to build something from scratch. Leads typically start coming in within 3–6 months as the site gains traction in local search. We give you a transparent update every month on where things stand.
Is there a long-term contract?
No contracts, ever. Cancel anytime with a simple email — no penalties, no hassle. We earn your business month-to-month by delivering results.
Do I own the website?
We build and own the website — think of it like exclusive access to a digital asset we maintain for you. You're not buying real estate; you're renting the leads it generates. That's what keeps your cost low and removes any upfront build cost. Every call and form fill goes to you and only you, for as long as you're a partner.
How is this different from Angi or HomeAdvisor?
They sell the same lead to 3–5 of your competitors at the same time, so you're bidding against others who got the exact same contact. We build a digital presence that sends leads straight to you — exclusive, not shared, not resold.
